Privacy Policy

HotkeyAI for macOS · Last updated November 23, 2026

HotkeyAI does not collect, store or share your personal data. The developer runs no servers and receives no information from the app: no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

Your text

When you press an action's shortcut, HotkeyAI copies the text from the field you are typing in and sends it, with that action's prompt, to the AI provider you chose for the action:

  • Apple Intelligence: processed on your Mac. Nothing leaves the device.
  • ChatGPT (sign-in) or OpenAI API: sent directly from your Mac to OpenAI over an encrypted connection, under your own account. See OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Claude (Anthropic API): sent directly to Anthropic. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Gemini (Google API): sent directly to Google. See Google's privacy policy.

The result replaces the text in your field. HotkeyAI keeps no copy of your text or of the results.

Stored on your Mac

  • Your actions, prompts, shortcuts and settings, in the app's own preferences.
  • API keys and ChatGPT sign-in tokens, in your macOS Keychain. You can remove them in HotkeyAI → Settings → AI Providers.

Clipboard and keyboard

HotkeyAI uses the clipboard for a moment to copy and paste, then restores what it contained before. With your permission it presses ⌘A, ⌘C and ⌘V in the field you are typing in, only when you press one of your shortcuts. It does not read your screen or record your typing.

Children

HotkeyAI is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

HotkeyAI не собирает, не хранит и не передаёт ваши персональные данные: у разработчика нет серверов, аналитики и рекламы. Текст, к которому вы применяете действие, отправляется только выбранному вами AI-провайдеру — напрямую с вашего Mac и под вашим аккаунтом; с Apple Intelligence он вообще не покидает устройство. Ключи и данные входа хранятся в связке ключей macOS. Буфер обмена используется на мгновение и затем восстанавливается.
