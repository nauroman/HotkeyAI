Privacy Policy

HotkeyAI for macOS and Windows · Last updated November 23, 2026

HotkeyAI does not collect, store or share your personal data. The developer runs no servers and receives no information from the app: no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

Your text

When you press an action's shortcut, HotkeyAI copies the text from the field you are typing in and sends it, with that action's prompt, to the AI provider you chose for the action:

  • Apple Intelligence (Mac only): processed on your Mac. Nothing leaves the device.
  • ChatGPT (sign-in) or OpenAI API: sent directly from your computer to OpenAI over an encrypted connection, under your own account. See OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Claude (Anthropic API): sent directly to Anthropic. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Gemini (Google API): sent directly to Google. See Google's privacy policy.

The result replaces the text in your field. HotkeyAI keeps no copy of your text or of the results.

Stored on your computer

  • Your actions, prompts, shortcuts and settings, in the app's own preferences.
  • API keys and ChatGPT sign-in tokens: on a Mac in your macOS Keychain; on Windows encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI), so only your Windows account can read them. You can remove them in HotkeyAI → Settings → AI Providers.

Clipboard and keyboard

HotkeyAI uses the clipboard for a moment to copy and paste, then restores what it contained before. It presses ⌘A, ⌘C and ⌘V on a Mac (with your permission), or Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V on Windows, in the field you are typing in, only when you press one of your shortcuts. On Windows the result it pastes is kept out of clipboard history. It does not read your screen or record your typing.

Children

HotkeyAI is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

HotkeyAI не собирает, не хранит и не передаёт ваши персональные данные: у разработчика нет серверов, аналитики и рекламы. Текст, к которому вы применяете действие, отправляется только выбранному вами AI-провайдеру — напрямую с вашего компьютера и под вашим аккаунтом; с Apple Intelligence (на Mac) он вообще не покидает устройство. Ключи и данные входа хранятся в связке ключей macOS или, в Windows, зашифрованы средствами Windows (DPAPI) для вашей учётной записи. Буфер обмена используется на мгновение и затем восстанавливается.
